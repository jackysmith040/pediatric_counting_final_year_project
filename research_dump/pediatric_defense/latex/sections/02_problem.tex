\begin{frame}{Problem Statement: The Invisible Child}
    \begin{columns}
        \begin{column}{0.55\textwidth}
            \textbf{The Algorithmic Blind Spot:}
            \begin{itemize}
                \item To automate counting, standard AI (like basic YOLO) relies on \textbf{Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS)}.
                \item In Ghana, mothers frequently carry infants on their backs using traditional \textit{ntoma}.
                \item \textbf{Geometric Failure:} NMS merges overlapping objects. The mother and child's bounding boxes fuse into one.
                \item The algorithm actively deletes the child, considering them "visual noise." Statistically, the child becomes invisible.
            \end{itemize}
        \end{column}
