            \begin{itemize}
                \item Counting pediatric patients requires distinguishing children from adults in highly dense crowds.
                \item Standard AI fails in extreme density.
                \item Whether a child is walking close to an adult or carried in an \textit{ntoma}, standard systems merge them into a single adult count.
