    \begin{block}{Allometric Scaling ($R$)}
        The human head-to-body ratio is a physical invariant.
        \begin{equation*}
            R = \frac{H_{total}}{H_{head}}
        \end{equation*}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Adult:} $R \approx 7.5 - 8.0$
            \item \textbf{Infant:} $R \approx 4.0 - 6.0$
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
    By calculating $R$ for every detected entity, we biologically verify age without facial recognition.
\end{frame}
