\chapter{Methodology}

\section{System Architecture}
The proposed system follows a modular pipeline:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Ingestion:} Low-res $640 \times 480$ clinical feed.
    \item \textbf{Detection \& Segmentation:} YOLOv11-seg identifies person masks.
    \item \textbf{Validation (Ada-Haar):} A Haar Cascade classifier validates pediatric head signatures to ensure high confidence.
    \item \textbf{Classification (Allometry):} Calculation of the $R$ ratio for each candidate.
    \item \textbf{Tracking (DeepSORT):} ID maintenance using Kalman Filters.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Handling Spatial Occlusion}
When a single body bounds two distinct heads ($h_1, h_2$), the system evaluates:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Vertical Displacement: $y_{h2} < y_{h1}$.
    \item Area Proportion: $A_{h2} < A_{h1}$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Edge Optimization}
We utilize Region of Interest (RoI) pooling to prioritize pediatric candidates, maintaining 30+ FPS on edge hardware (Liu et al., 2020).
