\chapter*{Abstract}
This research addresses the systemic ``Invisible Child'' phenomenon in Ghanaian pediatric departments, where cultural carrying practices (infants on mothers' backs) lead to significant demographic undercounting. We propose a hybrid mathematical framework that synthesizes scale-invariant Allometric Scaling with YOLOv11-seg instance segmentation and Kalman-filtered DeepSORT tracking. By utilizing biological constants (Head-to-Body ratios), the system mathematically separates occluded children from caregivers in low-resolution $640 \times 480$ clinical feeds. Calibrated against the Child Detection Dataset (CDD), the model aims to minimize Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) to $<10\%$, providing a privacy-preserving Force Multiplier for hospital administrators at KATH.
