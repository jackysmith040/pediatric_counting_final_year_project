\section{Background: The Pediatric Census Mission}

\begin{frame}{The Demographic Blindspot}
    In major Ghanaian hospitals like KATH, optimizing medical staff allocation requires precise demographic data.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Systemic Blindness:} LHIMS captures only primary patients, leaving infants statistically invisible \parencite{addotey2023}.
        \item \textbf{Research Gap:} General YOLO models fail in specialized medical scenes where infant morphology differs from adult signatures \parencite{diop2025}.
        \item \textbf{The Data Deficit:} This documented influx skews hospital statistics and exacerbates triage wait times \parencite{osei2024}.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
