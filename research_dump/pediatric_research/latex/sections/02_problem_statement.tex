\section{Problem Statement}

\begin{frame}{The Failure of Standard Geometry}
    Current Computer Vision (YOLO/SSD) is trained on Western commercial datasets. 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Identity Erasure:} High-density overlapping causes individual bounding boxes to merge.
        \item \textbf{The Invisible Child:} Infants on backs are mathematically absorbed into adult segments.
    \end{itemize}
    \begin{block}{The Research Question}
        How do we move from simple ``Box Detection'' to a \textbf{Biological Signature} that works in low-resolution clinical environments?
    \end{block}
\end{frame}
