\section{Problem Statement}

\begin{frame}{The Failure of Standard Surveillance}
    Currently, hospitals rely heavily on manual tallying, which is highly susceptible to human error and lacks the real-time statistical reliability needed for dynamic operations.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Geometric Flaw:} Standard surveillance algorithms interpret overlapping mother-child geometries as a single adult entity \parencite{ouyang2015}.
        \item \textbf{NMS Limits:} Standard Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) actively merges these overlapping shapes, discarding the carried child as visual noise.
        \item \textbf{Privacy Concerns:} Traditional surveillance often stores video on centralized servers, violating strict medical privacy standards.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
