# 6. Conclusion & Contribution

## Synthesis
This research bridges the gap between general computer vision models and the specialized requirements of pediatric healthcare in developing regions. By combining **DeepSORT tracking** with **Allometric Scaling**, we provide a mathematically defensible method for counting "invisible" infants in crowded OPDs.

## Key Contributions
- **Theoretical:** Integration of biological scaling laws into object detection pipelines.
- **Practical:** A real-time counting tool for hospital administrators to optimize triage and resource allocation.
- **Societal:** Improving pediatric data visibility in emerging economies.
