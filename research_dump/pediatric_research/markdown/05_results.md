# 5. Expected Results

## Metrics for Success
The performance will be evaluated using the following metrics:
- **mAP (mean Average Precision):** Targeting >0.85 for head detection.
- **MOTA (Multi-Object Tracking Accuracy):** Measuring ID persistence during crowd density shifts.
- **Counting Error (MAE):** Measuring the delta between manual clicks and automated counts.

## Projected Outcomes
1.  **Reduction in Undercounting:** The Allometric model is expected to reduce infant undercounts by up to **35%** compared to standard YOLO models.
2.  **Real-Time Efficiency:** Achieving >30 FPS on edge-computing devices (e.g., Jetson Nano/OAK-D).
3.  **Contextual Robustness:** Maintaining accuracy across varying African clinical lighting and background noise.
