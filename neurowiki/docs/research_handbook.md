*Codifying the standards of excellence for the Director's long-term research career.*

## 1. The Literature Review Protocol
- **Source Integrity:** Never rely on AI summaries alone. Always cross-reference with the original PDF.
- **Synthesis over Summary:** Don't just list what authors did; analyze why their methodology succeeded or failed in a local context (e.g., Ghana OPD).

## 2. Mathematical Rigor
- **The "No Black Box" Rule:** If a model (like YOLO) is used, the researcher must be able to explain the loss function, the matrix operations, and the bounding box logic.
- **Proof-of-Concept:** Always build a minimal working prototype (Axon's role) before scaling to complex architectures.

## 3. The Dissertation Standard (LaTeX Mirroring)
- Every significant finding is documented in the Mirror Directory (`research_dump/pediatric_research/`).
- Markdown is for the "Intuitive Brain" (Evie).
- LaTeX is for the "Formal Brain" (Axon).

## 4. Defensibility
- A researcher must be their own harshest critic.
- Use the **Rival Mode** in your Co-Brain to find holes in your own logic before your supervisors do.
