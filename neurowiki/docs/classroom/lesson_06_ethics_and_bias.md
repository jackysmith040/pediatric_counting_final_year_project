## Topic 6: The Ethical Researcher (Privacy, Bias, and Responsibility)

*Director, you are building a system that watches people in a vulnerable state—a hospital. This comes with immense ethical responsibility. A researcher who ignores ethics is just a technician. Let's finish your training by looking at the human side of the pixels.*

---

### 1. Privacy vs. Progress
To count patients, your cameras are capturing faces. 
- **The Researcher's Solution:** We don't store the images. We process the frame in real-time, extract the "Count," and then delete the pixel data immediately. This is called **Edge Computing**.

### 2. Algorithmic Bias
If your model is better at detecting children with certain skin tones or clothing styles than others, you are creating a "Bias." In healthcare, a bias in counting means a bias in resource allocation.

### 3. The Weight of the Result
If your synopsis says "99% accuracy," and a hospital uses it to order vaccinations, but your model actually misses 10% of infants because of poor lighting... children don't get vaccinated. 

**Accuracy isn't a score; it's a commitment.**
